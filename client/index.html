The store computer

Walk to the computer and press E. There you order stock, unlock new products, buy furniture and plants (delivered in boxes), set prices, hire staff, pay your bills and read messages from customers.

Prices & the register

Every shelf has price labels. Look at one and press E (or click it) to open the price calculator: what you pay, what you charge and your profit. At the register, click each product on the belt to scan it, then take the payment: give the right change from the cash drawer, or type the total on the card terminal.

Bakery, carts & security

Bakery: order dough, put the box in the oven, take the fresh bread out and stock the bread rack. Carts: take a shopping cart outside the entrance to move 4 boxes at once. Shoplifters: stop anyone marked SHOPLIFTER before the door with the baton, or hire a security guard. Promotions: start one from a shelf label's price calculator.

Fridges & counters

Open fridge doors (E) before stocking them. At the butcher and deli: take the product from the big fridge, cut it at the station, hand it over at the counter.

Settings

Low: fastest, no shadows. Medium: shadows. High: adds soft shading and glow (needs a good graphics card).

Helps fix bugs. Only a random id, web or app, the game version and error messages are sent: no name, no account, nothing personal.

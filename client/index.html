    🏪 The loop
    1. Build shelves, fridges and a checkout (B).
    2. Order products from suppliers (O). Trucks drop boxes at the yellow loading area.
    3. Carry boxes inside (E) and stock the right fixture — milk goes in a fridge!
    4. Open the store. Stand on the blue STAFF mat behind a checkout and press E to work the register.
    5. Hire staff (H) to automate jobs, and set prices (P) for profit.
    6. At closing time read the daily report. Don't stay in debt for 3 days!
    7. Level up to unlock produce, frozen food, the butcher, the deli, expansions and more.

    Tip: split up! One player runs the till, another hauls deliveries, another builds and orders.
